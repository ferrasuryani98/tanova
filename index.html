Profile

TANOVA is a Jakarta-born and raised & NYC-based DJ, electronic artist & producer, and vocalist.

She crafts beats that slide from vibrant future-bounce hooks into progressive grooves. Blending Future Bounce, Progressive House, and Bass House, her signature sound creates modern dance records that move between energetic yet bouncy intensity and futuristic feels to throwback/nostalgic anthems.
